# 10 - Long-distance connections
# A buck converter power stage with a feedback divider from the output all
# the way back to the controller, plus an enable line skipping stages. The
# long nets must be easy to follow and must not cut through other stages.

Input: DC Jack 12V
  pins: V+, V-

Controller: TPS54331 Buck Controller
  pins: VIN, EN, BOOT, SW, FB, COMP, GND

L1: inductor 22uH
  pins: 1, 2

Cout: capacitor 47uF
  pins: 1, 2

Load: 5V Load
  pins: VIN, GND, PGOOD

Rtop: resistor 52.3k
  pins: 1, 2

Rbot: resistor 10k
  pins: 1, 2

Input.V+ -> Controller.VIN : VIN_12V
Controller.SW -> L1.1 : SW_NODE
L1.2 -> Cout.1, Load.VIN, Rtop.1 : VOUT_5V
Rtop.2 -> Rbot.1, Controller.FB : FB
Load.PGOOD -> Controller.EN : PGOOD_EN
Input.V- -> Controller.GND, Cout.2, Load.GND, Rbot.2 : GND
